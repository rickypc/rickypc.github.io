### Abhiṣekapūjā

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    The prayer of [empowerment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abhi%E1%B9%A3eka_(Buddhism)) is here
    offered as a [gift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offering_(Buddhism)) to the sacred objects of
    practice. Offering the prayer in this way presents the empowerment itself as an act of
    generosity directed towards those objects, which are honoured as the supports of practice.
