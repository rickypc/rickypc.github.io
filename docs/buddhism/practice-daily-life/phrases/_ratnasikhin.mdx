### Ratnaśikhin Hṛdaya

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    Ratnaśikhin, Jewel Crest, is a Buddha of the past invoked in Tibetan Buddhist homage liturgies;
    his name combines ratna, jewel, and śikhin, one who has a crest or topknot. He made the
    aspiration that anyone in the world-realms of the ten directions who hears his name at the
    moment of death will, after passing, be reborn as a
    [deva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deva_(Buddhism)) in
    [Trāyastriṃśa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tr%C4%81yastri%E1%B9%83%C5%9Ba). His vow is
    remembered as a source of comfort and confidence for those approaching death, offering a
    connection to virtue, clarity, and an auspicious rebirth.

    <em>Source: [Homage to the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas](https://www.lotsawahouse.org/tibetan-masters/trulshik-rinpoche/homage-buddhas-and-bodhisattvas) - Lotsawa House</em>
