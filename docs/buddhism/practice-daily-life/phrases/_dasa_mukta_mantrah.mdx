### Daśa Mukta Mantrāḥ

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    Daśa Mukta Mantrāḥ, the Ten Liberation Prayers
    ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 十解脱咒), is a collection
    of [esoteric Buddhist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vajrayana) prayers recited for those near
    the end of life. The ten are
    [Amoghapāśa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avalokite%C5%9Bvara#Manifestations) Padma Uṣṇīṣa
    ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 白蓮花頂髻無垢密咒);
    [Darśana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darshan_(Indian_religions)#Mahayana_Buddhism) Mukta
    ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 見即解脱咒);
    [Samantabhadra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samantabhadra_(Bodhisattva)#Tibetan_Buddhism)
    Ṣaḍja Mukta ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 听即解脱咒);
    Mahāmaṇi Vipulavimāna ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters):
    大寶廣博樓閣善住祕密陀羅尼經); and [Akṣobhya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akshobhya)
    ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters):
    [阿閦佛](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E9%98%BF%E9%96%A6%E4%BD%9B)) Hṛdaya.

    The remaining prayers are Ratnaśikhin
    ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters):
    [宝髻佛](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E5%AE%9D%E9%AB%BB%E4%BD%9B)) Hṛdaya;
    [Bhaiṣajyaguru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhaisajyaguru)
    ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters):
    [藥師佛](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%97%A5%E5%B8%AB%E4%BD%9B)) Hṛdaya;
    [Uṣṇīṣavijayā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usnisavijaya)
    ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters):
    [佛頂尊勝佛母](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E5%B0%8A%E5%8B%9D%E4%BD%9B%E9%A0%82));
    [Avalokiteśvara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avalokite%C5%9Bvara)
    ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters):
    [觀世音](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E8%A7%80%E4%B8%96%E9%9F%B3%E8%8F%A9%E8%96%A9)) Hṛdaya; and
    [Amitābha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amit%C4%81bha)
    ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters):
    [阿彌陀佛](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E9%98%BF%E5%BC%A5%E9%99%80%E4%BD%9B)) Hṛdaya. Reciting
    these prayers is held to aid the dying and the deceased.
  </summary>
