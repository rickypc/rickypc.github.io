### Raśmi Vimala Viśuddha Prabhā Nāma Hṛdaya

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    Raśmi Vimala Viśuddha Prabhā Nāma Hṛdaya is the heart-recitation of
    the longer dhāraṇī, and it also purifies the
    [five uninterrupted negative karmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anantarika-karma).

    If this prayer is placed inside a stupa, then any being - including
    difficult transmigratory beings - who sees the stupa, hears of it,
    touches it, or is touched by dust or wind that comes from it, is freed
    from all negative karmas. They will be reborn in the realms of fortunate
    transmigratory beings and will not fall into the lower realms.

    If one offers even a bell to a stupa containing this prayer, all the
    sentient beings in that area - animals or humans - by hearing the
    sound of that bell are completely purified of the five uninterrupted
    negative karmas. Even just thinking about the stupa purifies the
    five uninterrupted negative karmas.
  </summary>
