### Dhanadatārā

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    Dhanadatārā is a manifestation of [Tārā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tara_(Buddhism))
    associated with granting success, ultimate happiness, and enough financial resources to live
    with dignity and generosity. Her name combines the Sanskrit words for wealth and giving with
    Tārā, the saviouress.

    The Hundred and Eight Names of the Goddess Tārā praises her for making wealth increase and merit
    ever-victorious, and she is invoked for material support and success in undertakings.

    <em>Source: [The Hundred and Eight Names of the Goddess Tārā](https://84000.co/translation/toh728) - 84000: Translating the Words of the Buddha</em>
