### Pañca Jambhala

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    The [five Jambhalas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jambhala#Five_Jambhalas) are forms of
    Jambhala, the deity of wealth in Tibetan Buddhism, honoured in rituals for prosperity and
    enrichment. They are manifestations of the compassion of
    [Buddhas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhas) and
    [Bodhisattvas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodhisattva), guiding [sentient
    beings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentient_beings_(Buddhism)) along the path to
    [enlightenment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enlightenment_in_Buddhism). They carry the essence
    of generosity and represent the activity of increasing benefit; their aspiration is to help the
    poor and those suffering from ill-fortune, and they are invoked to relieve poverty and support
    Dharma practice.

    <em>Source: [Jambhala Series](https://www.lotsawahouse.org/topics/jambhala/) - Lotsawa House</em>
  </summary>
