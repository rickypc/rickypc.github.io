### Mahāvairocana Sarvadurgati Pariśodhana Dhāraṇī

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    [Vairocana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vairocana) is seen as the
    [dharmakāya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharmak%C4%81ya) - the supreme
    [buddha-body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trikaya), the body of ultimate reality - and the
    embodiment of the Buddhist concept of wisdom and purity. In the conception of the [Five
    Jinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_Tathagatas) of
    [Mahayana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahayana) and
    [Vajrayana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vajrayana) Buddhism, Vairocana sits at the centre and
    is often considered a [Primordial Buddha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adi-Buddha), and in East
    Asian esoteric Buddhism Mahāvairocana is regarded as a cosmic Buddha whose body is the entire
    universe, the [Dharmadhātu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharmadhatu). The Mahāvairocana
    Sarvadurgati Pariśodhana Dhāraṇī is a purification dhāraṇī centred on him. Its Sanskrit title
    names the purification of all bad rebirths, and the tantra to which it belongs is classified
    among the Yoga tantras of the Tibetan canon. The mantra is said to purify negative karma, avert
    lower rebirths, and cleanse obscurations.

    <em>Source: [The Tantra Purifying Bad Rebirths](https://84000.co/translation/toh483) - 84000: Translating the Words of the Buddha</em>
