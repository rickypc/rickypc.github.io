### Catvāry Apramāṇāni

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    Catvāry Apramāṇāni, the [four immeasurables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahmavihara), are
    [maitrī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maitr%C4%AB) (loving-kindness),
    [karuṇā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karu%E1%B9%87%C4%81#Buddhism) (compassion),
    [muditā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mudita) (sympathetic joy), and
    [upekṣā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upek%E1%B9%A3%C4%81) (equanimity) - the boundless
    qualities of heart that the bodhisattva cultivates without limit.

    In practice they are extended without limit toward all sentient beings. The traditional prayer
    expresses the wish that beings meet with happiness, be free from suffering, and remain
    undisturbed. Cultivating them is held to calm the mind and support the bodhisattva path.

    <em>Source: [An Extensive Commentary on the Four Immeasurables](https://www.lotsawahouse.org/indian-masters/buddhagupta/four-immeasurables-commentary) - Lotsawa House</em>
  </summary>
