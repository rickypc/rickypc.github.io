### Nīlakaṇṭha Dhāraṇī

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    The most widely practiced [Far East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_East) version,
    cross-checked with the [Dunhuang manuscript
    S.231](https://idp.bl.uk/collection/469DEE0D155C48D2945C912ED9ADB21D/), is known as the Great
    Compassion Dhāraṇī ([Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit_language): महाकारुणिक
    धारणी, [Romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Sanskrit): Mahākāruṇika
    Dhāraṇī, [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 大悲咒). It is a
    long spell associated with Avalokiteśvara and prominent in East Asian Buddhism, and its Sanskrit
    name means Blue-Necked. It is held to bring protection, purification, and healing, and to embody
    the boundless compassion of Avalokiteśvara; it is often used on behalf of the sick and the
    dying.
