### Avalokiteśvara Hṛdaya

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    [Avalokiteśvara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avalokite%C5%9Bvara) is a bodhisattva of
    compassion venerated throughout the Buddhist world, and the Avalokiteśvara Hṛdaya is the
    six-syllable mantra <code>{phrase.transliteration.children}</code>, understood as Jewel-Lotus.
    While [Avalokiteśvara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avalokite%C5%9Bvara) was depicted as male
    in India, in [East Asian Buddhism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Asian_Buddhism)
    [Avalokiteśvara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avalokite%C5%9Bvara) is most often shown as a
    female figure known as [Guanyin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guanyin). The Buddha taught that
    recitation of this prayer while focusing on the sound can lead to the attainment of eight
    hundred [samādhis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samadhi), and the dhāraṇī text states that
    reciting the mantra purifies negative karma and prevents lower rebirths.

    <em>Source: [The Dhāraṇī of Avalokiteśvara](https://84000.co/translation/toh910) - 84000: Translating the Words of the Buddha</em>
