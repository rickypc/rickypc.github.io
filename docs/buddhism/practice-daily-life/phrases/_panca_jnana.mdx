### Pañcajñāna

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    Pañcajñāna, the [five wisdoms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_wisdoms), is a Mahāyāna and
    Vajrayāna schema describing the awakened mind. The set comprises the mirror-like, the
    equanimous, the discriminating, the all-accomplishing, and the dharmadhātu wisdoms - also
    rendered as the wisdom of equality, discerning wisdom, all-accomplishing wisdom, and the wisdom
    of dharmadhātu. A short mantra, <code>{phrase.transliteration.children}</code>, is recited as a
    condensation of these five aspects, a short prayer that gathers the mind into the fullness of
    the awakened view.
