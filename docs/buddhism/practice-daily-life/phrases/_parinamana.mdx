### Pariṇāmanā

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    Pariṇāmanā is the [dedication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transfer_of_merit) of merit, a
    short closing that gathers the goodness of the practice and offers it freely to all beings, that
    they too may come to peace. The term conveys the turning or directing of merit toward a chosen
    aim rather than retaining it; a common Tibetan prayer of four lines asks that by this merit all
    beings attain enlightenment. The practice closes a session and is held to protect and multiply
    the merit thus offered for the welfare of all beings.

    <em>Source: [Dedication of Merit](https://www.lotsawahouse.org/words-of-the-buddha/dedication-sonam-di-yi) - Lotsawa House</em>
