### Āryatārā Aṣṭaghora Tāraṇī Sūtra

[Tārā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tara_(Buddhism)) who protects from the
[Eight Dangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tara_(Buddhism)#As_a_popular_saviour_deity).

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    The Āryatārā Aṣṭaghora Tāraṇī Sūtra presents
    [Tārā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tara_(Buddhism)) explaining that the fate of each being is
    the result of past negative actions and that virtuous conduct is the only way to avoid suffering
    in the future. She describes the path to liberation through a series of evocative metaphors and
    offers a sacred [dhāraṇī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharani) as a means to help others
    achieve liberation from [saṃsāra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sa%E1%B9%83s%C4%81ra). She also
    outlines for recitation a praise of her myriad qualities, in particular her ability to protect
    beings from the [eight
    dangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tara_(Buddhism)#As_a_popular_saviour_deity) -
    traditionally lions, elephants, fire, snakes, robbers, floods, disease, and demons. The text
    presents Tārā as a saviouress who guards practitioners from such perils, outer and inner.

    <em>Source: [Introduction to Tārā](https://www.lotsawahouse.org/topics/tara/intro) - Lotsawa House</em>
  </summary>
