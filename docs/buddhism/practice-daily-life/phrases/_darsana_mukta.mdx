### Darśana Mukta

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    Darśana Mukta, [Liberation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enlightenment_in_Buddhism) by seeing,
    is a Tibetan Buddhist prayer held to purify 100,000
    [eons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeon#Philosophy_and_mysticism) of negative karma
    ([Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit_language): पापकर्मन्,
    [Romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Sanskrit): pāpakarman) merely by being
    seen.

    Its Sanskrit title pairs darśana, seeing or beholding, with mukta, freed or liberated. Both its
    recitation and its sight are traditionally linked with purification and with support for
    liberation.
  </summary>
