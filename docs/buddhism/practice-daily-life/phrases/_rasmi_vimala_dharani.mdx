### Raśmi Vimala Viśuddha Prabhā Nāma Dhāraṇī

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    The Raśmi Vimala Viśuddha Prabhā Nāma Dhāraṇī, whose English title is The Dhāraṇī "Stainless
    Rays of Pure Light", purifies the [five uninterrupted negative
    karmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anantarika-karma); its name means rays of stainless and
    pure light.

    If this prayer is placed inside a stupa, then any being - including difficult transmigratory
    beings - who sees the stupa, hears of it, touches it, or is touched by dust or wind that comes
    from it, is freed from all negative karmas. They will be reborn in the realms of fortunate
    transmigratory beings and will not fall into the lower realms.

    If one offers even a bell to a stupa containing this prayer, all the sentient beings in that
    area - animals or humans - by hearing the sound of that bell are completely purified of the five
    uninterrupted negative karmas. Even just thinking about the stupa purifies the five
    uninterrupted negative karmas.

    <em>Source: [The Dhāraṇī "Stainless Rays of Pure Light"](https://84000.co/translation/toh510) - 84000 Translating the Words of the Buddha</em>
  </summary>
