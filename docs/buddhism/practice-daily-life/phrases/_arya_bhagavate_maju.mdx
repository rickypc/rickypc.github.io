### Ārya Bhagavate Majū Hṛdaya

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    Ārya Bhagavate Majū Hṛdaya is a devotional invocation associated with
    [Majū](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazu)
    ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters):
    [媽祖](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E5%AA%BD%E7%A5%96)), who was traditionally thought to roam
    the seas and to protect her believers through miraculous interventions. She is now generally
    regarded by her believers as a powerful and benevolent Queen of Heaven
    ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters): 天上聖母), and this
    heart-mantra invokes her protecting presence. Its Sanskrit line, `oṃ ārya sūrya tatama svāhā`,
    is a short mantra-like offering addressed to her, and devotees are said to recite it seeking
    safety at sea and general blessings.

    <em>Source: [Mazu Worship in Singapore](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-21-issue-1-apr-jun-2025-mazu-goddess-of-the-sea-worship-birthday-thian-hock-keng/) - BiblioAsia, National Library Board Singapore</em>
  </summary>
