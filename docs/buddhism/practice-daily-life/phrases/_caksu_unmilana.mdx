### Cakṣu Unmīlana

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    Cakṣu Unmīlana, the [opening of the
    eyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddh%C4%81bhi%E1%B9%A3eka), is a brief rite of consecration
    in which the awakened gaze of a sacred image is established, inviting the wisdom beings to dwell
    within it. In the Tibetan consecration sequence, the rite follows the bathing and empowerment of
    the object.

    Those who behold the image are said thereby to meet the awakened mind directly.

    <em>Source: [Consecration Series](https://www.lotsawahouse.org/topics/consecration/) - Lotsawa House</em>
