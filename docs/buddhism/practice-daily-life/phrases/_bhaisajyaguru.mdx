### Bhaiṣajyaguru Hṛdaya

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    [Bhaiṣajyaguru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhaisajyaguru), the Medicine Buddha, is the
    [Buddha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhahood) of healing and medicine in [Mahāyāna
    Buddhism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahayana) and the Vajrayāna traditions, curing suffering
    ([duḥkha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Du%E1%B8%A5kha)) with the medicine of his teachings. His
    name means Medicine Master, and his heart dhāraṇī invokes him by that name; reciting it is
    understood to support healing and the relief of suffering.

    <em>Source: [The Essence Dhāraṇī of the Tathāgata Bhaiṣajyaguru](https://84000.co/translation/toh862) - 84000: Translating the Words of the Buddha</em>
