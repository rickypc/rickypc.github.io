### Bodhigarbhālaṃkāralakṣa

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    The Bodhigarbhālaṃkāralakṣa is a dhāraṇī whose title names a hundred thousand ornaments of the
    essence of awakening. Placing even one prayer of it inside a
    [stupa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stupa) is promised the same merit as building 100,000
    [stupas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stupa) - whether that
    [stupa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stupa) is gigantic like [Bodh
    Gayā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodh_Gaya) or tiny as a finger. Dedicating the merit to
    sentient beings, for their happiness up to full enlightenment, is said to bring them
    unbelievable benefits and the practitioner unbelievable merit.

    Offering to such a [stupa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stupa) is understood to reach beyond
    the physical structure, becoming an offering to all
    [Buddhas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddha), all
    [Dharma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist_paths_to_liberation), and all
    [Sangha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangha) - the [Three Rare Sublime
    Ones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refuge_in_Buddhism) - in the ten directions and in any
    universe.

    Historically the dhāraṇī functioned as a "secret relic", a substitute for the Buddha's bodily
    relics. It appears widely across India, Southeast Asia, and Afghanistan, often stamped onto clay
    tablets and sealings found at sacred sites such as Bodh Gayā, frequently alongside the
    [Vimaloṣṇīṣa](index.mdx#vimala-uṣṇīṣa) and [Ye Dharmā](index.mdx#pratītyasamutpāda-gāthā)
    formulas. Its use reflects a major consecration tradition in which dhāraṇī texts empower stupas
    and images, generating merit equivalent to reciting vast scriptures and establishing a direct
    connection with both mundane and transcendent maṇḍalas.

    <em>Source: [The Dhāraṇī "One Hundred Thousand Ornaments of the Essence of Awakening"](https://84000.co/translation/toh509) - 84000 Translating the Words of the Buddha</em>
  </summary>
