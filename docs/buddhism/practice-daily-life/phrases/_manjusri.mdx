### Mañjuśrī Hṛdaya

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    Mañjuśrī is a bodhisattva associated with
    [wisdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Praj%C4%B1%C4%81_(Buddhism)) and insight in Mahāyāna and
    Vajrayāna Buddhism, and his name is often translated as Gentle Glory. His heart mantra is
    <code>{phrase.transliteration.children}</code>, and this prayer opens the five
    [Dharma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharma#Buddha's_teachings) doors. The five syllables are
    the opening of the arapacana, the syllabary the
    [Prajñāpāramitā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prajnaparamita) uses to spell out the whole of
    the teaching, and they stand at the head of its forty-two letter doors. Each syllable marks one
    door:

    > 1. **ā**dya-anutpannatvād: a door to the insight that all dharmas are unproduced from the very
         beginning.
    > 2. **ra**jas: a door to the insight that all dharmas are without dirt.
    > 3. **pa**ramārtha: a door to the insight that all dharmas have been expounded in the ultimate
         sense.
    > 4. **cya**vana: a door to the insight that the decrease or rebirth of any dharma cannot be
         apprehended, because all dharmas do not decrease, nor are they reborn.
    > 5. **nā**ma: a door to the insight that the names of all dharmas have vanished, so that the
         essential nature behind names can be neither gained nor lost.

    This prayer is believed to enhance
    [wisdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Praj%C4%B1%C4%81_(Buddhism)) and to improve skill in
    debating, memory, writing, and other
    [literary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit_Buddhist_literature) abilities. The sūtra
    itself promises twenty benefits to anyone who receives these letter doors, recites them, or
    explains them to others, and it opens that list with a strong memory, wisdom, and unhindered
    eloquence in teaching.

    <em>Source: [One Hundred and Eight Names of Mañjuśrī](https://84000.co/translation/toh642) - 84000: Translating the Words of the Buddha; [摩訶般若波羅蜜經 (T0223)](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T0223) - CBETA, Taishō Tripiṭaka</em>
  </summary>
