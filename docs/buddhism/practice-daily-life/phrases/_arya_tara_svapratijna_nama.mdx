### Āryatārā Svapratijñā Nāma Dhāraṇī

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

  <summary>
    The Āryatārā Svapratijñā Nāma Dhāraṇī is [Tārā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tara_(Buddhism))'s
    own promise - a dhāraṇī spoken in her voice, expressing the vows she has made on behalf of
    beings. Reciting it calls practitioners to remember and to rely upon that promise.
