### Vajrakīlāya Hṛdaya

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    A [wrathful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrathful_deities) form of
    [Vajrasattva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vajrasattva),
    [Vajrakīlaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vajrakilaya) (Dorje Phurba), also called Vajrakīla,
    is the yidam who embodies the unstoppable enlightened activity of all the Buddhas and is central
    to the Nyingma school. His practice is famed for cutting through the most stubborn obstacles,
    subduing harmful forces, and purifying the dense spiritual pollution of this degenerate age.
    With the [phurba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phurba) dagger that annihilates ignorance at its
    root, Vajrakīlaya manifests fierce compassion that clears the practitioner's path toward
    awakening.

    <em>Source: [Introduction to Vajrakīlaya](https://www.lotsawahouse.org/topics/vajrakilaya/intro) - Lotsawa House</em>
