### Guhyadhātukaraṇḍa

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    Guhyadhātukaraṇḍa is understood to purify the negative karma of the ten non-virtuous actions and
    to liberate beings from the [eight hot
    hells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naraka_(Buddhism)#Eight_Hot_narakas), including the
    unbearable hell ([Avīci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Av%C4%ABci#Details_about_Av%C4%ABci)).
    The prayer also purifies the [five uninterrupted negative
    karmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anantarika-karma).

    Printing this prayer just once is said to collect the same amount of merit as making offerings
    to 100,000 x 10 million x 100 billion Buddhas. The merit is described as vast as the number of
    Buddhas that could fit in ninety-nine sesame seed pods, and the practitioner is said to be
    guided by exactly that many Buddhas.

    <em>Source: [The Dhāraṇī for Secret Relics](https://84000.co/translation/toh507) - 84000 Translating the Words of the Buddha</em>
  </summary>
