### Pañca Jambhala

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    The [five Jambhalas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jambhala#Five_Jambhalas)
    are manifestations of the compassion of
    [Buddhas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhas) and
    [Bodhisattvas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodhisattva), guiding
    [sentient beings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentient_beings_(Buddhism))
    along the path to [enlightenment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enlightenment_in_Buddhism).
    They carry the essence of generosity and represent the activity of
    increasing benefit; their aspiration is to help the poor and those
    suffering from ill-fortune.
  </summary>
