### Vasuratna

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    Vasuratna, 'wealth-jewel', names a mantra invoking [Guru
    Rinpoche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padmasambhava) as Guru Jambhala, the wealth-bestowing
    manifestation arising from his Ratna Tötreng Tsal form - one of the five enlightened activities
    of Guru Rinpoche associated with prosperity, enrichment, and the removal of obstacles. Tötreng
    Tsal ('Powerful Garland of Skulls') is described in traditional sources as a secret name of Guru
    Rinpoche, invoked for swift enlightened action.

    In this jewel-aspect, Guru Rinpoche manifests as
    [Jambhala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jambhala), the deity of wealth honored in practices for
    prosperity, to dispel poverty, increase resources, and create the supportive conditions
    necessary for Dharma practice.

    <em>Source: [Jambhala Series](https://www.lotsawahouse.org/topics/jambhala/) - Lotsawa House</em>
  </summary>
