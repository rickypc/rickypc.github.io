### Śākyamuni Hṛdaya

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    The Śākyamuni Hṛdaya is the heart-essence of Buddha
    [Śākyamuni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Buddha#Siddh%C4%81rtha_Gautama_and_Buddha_Shakyamuni),
    the historical sage of the Śākya clan, known here as the Munīndra Hṛdaya Mantraḥ. Through simply
    hearing it, one is said to gather vast merit and purify karmic obscurations. When recited, it is
    understood to protect the practitioner from obstacles and to support the unfolding of wisdom.
    These qualities are taught in the abridged
    [Prajñāpāramitā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prajnaparamita), where this mantra is praised as
    the essence of the Buddha's liberating insight.

    <em>Source: [The Dhāraṇī of the Essence of Śākyamuni](https://84000.co/translation/toh533) - 84000: Translating the Words of the Buddha</em>
  </summary>
