### Vimala Uṣṇīṣa

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    The Vimala Uṣṇīṣa, or Stainless Uṣṇīṣa, is a mantra recited for deep purification and the
    removal of heavy negative karma, and is widely regarded in
    [Mahāyāna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahayana) and
    [Vajrayāna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vajrayana) traditions as one of the most powerful
    dhāraṇīs for consecration. Its name joins vimala, meaning stainless, with uṣṇīṣa, the crown
    protuberance of a buddha, and it is also known as the Great Wisdom Vimaloṣṇīṣa Dhāraṇī. It is
    praised for its ability to purify even the five extremely negative karmas upon hearing, and is
    traditionally used to bless and consecrate sacred images, environments, and stūpas.

    Historical evidence from sites such as
    [Ratnagiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ratnagiri,_Odisha) shows that copies of this dhāraṇī
    were placed inside [stūpas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stupa) for centuries, reflecting its
    long-standing role in Buddhist consecration practice. The Vimaloṣṇīṣa text itself states that
    inserting this dhāraṇī into a stūpa generates merit equivalent to filling it with the relics of
    countless Buddhas, making it one of the most revered dhāraṇīs for empowering and sanctifying
    holy objects.

    <em>Source: [The Dhāraṇī, "The Stainless Uṣṇīṣa"](https://84000.co/translation/toh599) - 84000 Translating the Words of the Buddha</em>
  </summary>
