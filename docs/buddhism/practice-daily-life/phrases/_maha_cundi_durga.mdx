### Mahā Cundī Durgā Dhāranī

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    [Cundī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cundi_(Buddhism)) is a female
    Indian [Buddhist deity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddhist_deities)
    who remains popular in [East Asian Buddhism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Asian_Buddhism).
    In [Chinese Buddhism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Buddhism),
    she is associated with the practice of the well-known Cundī
    [dhāraṇī](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharani), which is performed
    with a specific [mudra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mudra) (hand
    gesture) and the use of a circular mirror. She is considered able to
    purify negative karma, provide protection, and support spiritual
    practice so that one may quickly attain Buddhahood.
