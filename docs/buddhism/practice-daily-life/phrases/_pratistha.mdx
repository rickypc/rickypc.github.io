    practice. During the ritual the object is cleansed, empowered, and invited to receive the wisdom
    beings; the consecration prayer is recited to awaken the sacred objects of practice, so that the
    image, stupa, or text becomes a living vessel for the awakened qualities of body, speech, and
    mind. It precedes the object's use in worship and offering.
