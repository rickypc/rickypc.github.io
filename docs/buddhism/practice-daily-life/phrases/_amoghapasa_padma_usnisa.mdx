### Amoghapāśa Padma Uṣṇīṣa

<Phrase {...phrase} {...props} />

<details>
  <summary>
    A highly esoteric emanation of
    [Amoghapāśa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avalokite%C5%9Bvara#Manifestations). Whoever sees,
    hears, remembers, or touches this prayer is purified of all negativities and gains freedom from
    rebirth in the lower realms. The benefit is said to reach anyone who encounters the prayer
    through any of the senses or through memory, without distinction.

    <em>Source: [不空羂索神變真言經 (T1092)](https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/zh/T1092) - CBETA, Taishō Tripiṭaka</em>
